This review evaluates evidence quality and runtime boundaries, not schema shape alone. Empty fields are accepted when evidence is absent.

- **G — Forced schema filling:** no; values, decision patterns, epistemic state, and goals remain empty.

- **I — Dynamic/stable separation:** yes; D3 location and concerns remain blank, while name history and identity stay stable.
- **J — Depth as importance:** no; `full` controls available detail only and does not assert future relevance or scene frequency.
- **K — PC control:** not applicable to this NPC; her relationship record does not authorize control of 御临风.

Character-specific result: all three names are preserved with stage meaning; the 御临风 relation contains only recorded events; no automatic “信任／依恋” wording appears.
